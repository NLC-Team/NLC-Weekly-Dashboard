About this list

The dashboard has no sign-in, so a role here is a label only — it records who does what at the firm and does not restrict what anyone can see or change. Everyone who can open the dashboard can use every page.

This roster is also separate from the staff names on the Overview and Overdue pages: those come from the assignees in your Karbon import, so a person shows up there whether or not they are listed here.
